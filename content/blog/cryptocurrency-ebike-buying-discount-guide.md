Independent Electric Bikes accepts cryptocurrency payments — and offers a discount for using them. If you have never bought a physical product with crypto before, this guide walks you through the process, the discount, and why it works.

## Why We Offer a Crypto Discount

Credit card processors charge merchants 1.5–3 % per transaction. For a $3,000 e-bike, that is $45–$90 in fees before we even ship the product. Bank transfers avoid this but take 1–3 business days to clear, delaying your order.

Cryptocurrency payments settle in minutes with fees under $1 on most networks. We pass that saving directly to you as a discount on your order.

## Which Cryptocurrencies We Accept

We accept the major cryptocurrencies:

- **Bitcoin (BTC):** the original and most widely held. Transaction fees vary ($1–$10 depending on network congestion). Confirmation time: 10–60 minutes.
- **Ethereum (ETH):** the second-largest cryptocurrency. Fees (gas) vary significantly — can be under $1 or over $20 during peak periods. Confirmation time: 1–5 minutes.
- **USDT (Tether) and USDC:** stablecoins pegged to the US dollar. If you want to pay in crypto without exposure to price volatility during the transaction, stablecoins are the simplest option. Available on multiple networks (Ethereum, Tron, Polygon) — we will confirm which network to use.
- **Litecoin (LTC):** faster and cheaper than Bitcoin for payments. Fees typically under $0.05. Confirmation time: 2–5 minutes.

## How the Process Works

1. **Place your order** through our website or WhatsApp. Select cryptocurrency as your payment method.
2. **We send you a payment request** with the exact amount in your chosen cryptocurrency, a wallet address, and a QR code. The amount is calculated at the current exchange rate and includes your crypto discount.
3. **Send the payment** from your wallet (exchange account, hardware wallet, or mobile wallet). Double-check the wallet address — crypto transactions are irreversible.
4. **We confirm receipt** once the transaction has enough confirmations on the blockchain (typically 1–3 confirmations depending on the cryptocurrency and amount).
5. **Your order ships** as soon as payment is confirmed.

## How Much Is the Discount?

The crypto discount is listed on our website and applied automatically when you select cryptocurrency as your payment method. The exact percentage may vary — check the current rate on the product page or ask us via WhatsApp.

## Do I Need to Own Crypto Already?

No. If you want to take advantage of the discount but do not currently hold cryptocurrency, you can buy it specifically for this purchase. Australian-friendly exchanges include:

- **CoinSpot:** Australian-owned, AUSTRAC-registered, accepts PayID and bank transfer. Verification takes minutes to hours.
- **Swyftx:** Australian-based, competitive fees, wide coin selection.
- **Binance Australia:** large global exchange with Australian entity.
- **Kraken:** well-established international exchange with AUD support.

The process: sign up, verify your identity (required by Australian law), deposit AUD via bank transfer or PayID, buy the cryptocurrency, then send it to the wallet address we provide.

**Note:** exchange verification can take anywhere from 10 minutes to 48 hours depending on the platform and demand. If you want to pay with crypto, set up your exchange account before placing your order.

## Tax Implications

In Australia, the ATO treats cryptocurrency as property, not currency. When you spend crypto to buy an e-bike, you are technically "disposing" of the crypto — which may trigger a capital gains event if the crypto has increased in value since you acquired it.

**Example:** you bought 0.05 BTC for $2,000 AUD. By the time you spend it on an e-bike, that 0.05 BTC is worth $3,000 AUD. The $1,000 difference is a capital gain, and you may owe tax on it (reduced by 50 % if you held the crypto for more than 12 months).

If you buy crypto and spend it immediately (same day), the capital gain or loss is typically negligible.

**We are not tax advisors.** If you hold significant crypto positions, consult an accountant familiar with Australian cryptocurrency tax law before making large purchases.

## Security Tips

- **Double-check the wallet address** before sending. Copy-paste it; do not type it manually. Some malware replaces clipboard wallet addresses with the attacker's address.
- **Send a small test transaction first** if you are sending a large amount. Confirm it arrives, then send the remainder.
- **Use a reputable wallet.** Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor) are the safest. Mobile wallets (Trust Wallet, MetaMask) are convenient but less secure. Exchange wallets are fine for immediate purchases.
- **Never share your seed phrase or private keys** with anyone, including us. We will never ask for them. We only need you to send funds to a wallet address.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What if the price changes between placing the order and sending payment?**
We lock the exchange rate at the time we generate the payment request. You have a window (typically 15–30 minutes) to complete the payment at that rate. If the window expires, we generate a new request at the current rate.

**What if I send the wrong amount?**
Contact us immediately. If you overpaid, we will refund the difference in the same cryptocurrency. If you underpaid, we will let you know the remaining balance.

**Can I get a refund in crypto?**
Refunds for cryptocurrency payments are processed in the same cryptocurrency at the current exchange rate, or as a bank transfer in AUD — your choice.

**Is it safe?**
Blockchain transactions are secured by cryptography and are publicly verifiable. The risk is not in the payment method — it is in sending funds to the wrong address. Follow the security tips above and you will be fine.
