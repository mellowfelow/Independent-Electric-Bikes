A mobility scooter is only as useful as it is comfortable and safe to live with. The right accessories can turn a basic scooter into one that suits your day: a basket for the shopping, a cover to protect it from the Australian sun, lights so you are seen at dusk, and a few small comfort upgrades that make longer outings far more pleasant. This guide runs through the most useful mobility scooter accessories and what to think about before you buy.

## Start With What You Actually Need

It is tempting to buy a bundle of add-ons, but the best accessories solve a real problem. Think about your routine:

- Do you carry shopping or a handbag?
- Do you ride at dusk or in the evening?
- Do you park outside where the scooter is exposed to sun and rain?
- Do you find the seat or controls uncomfortable on longer trips?
- Do you worry about theft?

Match accessories to those needs and check compatibility with your model. Mounting points, tiller shapes, and seat designs differ, so confirm that an accessory fits before purchasing.

## Baskets and Carrying Solutions

Carrying things safely is one of the most common needs.

- **Front baskets** are convenient for shopping, bags, and everyday items. Look for a sturdy basket that attaches securely and does not block your view or interfere with steering.
- **Rear baskets or storage boxes** suit heavier or bulkier loads, and keep weight lower and more stable.
- **Under-seat or side bags** keep small items handy.
- **Walking stick or crutch holders** make it easier to keep mobility aids with you.
- **Drink holders** are a small but appreciated addition on longer rides.

Be mindful of weight. Overloading a basket can affect handling and exceed the scooter's rated limits, so stay within the manufacturer's guidance.

## Weather Covers

Australian conditions can be tough on a scooter. Sun fades and cracks seats and plastics, rain can damage electronics, and dust gets into controls.

- **Storage covers** protect the scooter when parked in a garage, carport, or outdoor area. Look for breathable, water-resistant material that fits your size of scooter.
- **Rider rain covers or ponchos** can help you stay dry on short trips, though riding in heavy rain is best avoided.
- **Control panel covers** protect the display and switches from moisture and sun.

Covers should be used on a dry, cool scooter, and the scooter should be dry before covering to avoid trapped moisture.

## Mirrors

A rear-view mirror helps you check behind you without twisting, which is especially useful on footpaths with cyclists, other scooters, and busy shopping strips. Choose a mirror that attaches firmly to the tiller or handlebar and can be adjusted without tools. Mirrors do not replace looking over your shoulder when it is safe, but they add useful awareness.

## Lights, Reflectors, and Flags

Being visible is a key part of staying safe.

- **Front and rear lights** are valuable at dusk, at night, and in poor weather. Some scooters include them, while others allow add-ons.
- **Reflectors** on the sides and rear help other road and path users see you.
- **Safety flags** on a tall pole make a low-profile scooter far more visible to drivers and cyclists, particularly near driveways and crossings.
- **Indicator lights** can help signal your intentions on busier paths.

Lighting rules and recommendations vary by state, so check with your state road authority or local council for what applies to you. Our [mobility scooter laws and footpath rules guide](/blog/mobility-scooter-laws-footpath-rules-australia/) explains where to look.

## Comfort Upgrades

If you spend a lot of time on your scooter, small comfort changes make a big difference.

- **Seat cushions or pads** can add support and reduce pressure on longer rides. Choose a cushion designed for your needs, and speak to an occupational therapist if you have pressure-care concerns.
- **Back supports** and lumbar cushions can improve posture.
- **Armrest pads** reduce discomfort on hands and forearms.
- **Tiller grips and handlebar covers** can make gripping easier and keep hands warm in cooler weather.
- **Footplate mats** add grip and comfort for feet.
- **Cold-weather muffs** keep hands warm and protected on winter outings.

If you are unsure what suits you, an occupational therapist can recommend options based on your health and mobility needs.

## Stability and Safety Aids

- **Anti-tip wheels** can add stability where fitted or compatible with your model. Check with the manufacturer.
- **Seat belts** are available for some riders and models. Ask your retailer or therapist if one suits you.
- **Horn or bell upgrades** make it easier for others to hear you coming.
- **Reflective strips** can be added to bags or clothing for extra visibility.

Always check that any safety accessory is compatible with your scooter and installed correctly.

## Security Accessories

A mobility scooter is valuable, and theft or tampering can leave you stranded.

- **Wheel or disc locks** discourage opportunistic theft and stop the scooter being rolled away.
- **Chain locks and cable locks** secure the frame to a fixed object when you leave it outside.
- **Ignition key discipline.** Always remove the key and keep it with you.
- **GPS trackers** may help locate a missing scooter, depending on the product.
- **Insurance** for theft and damage is worth considering. Check with your insurer.

Park in well-lit, visible areas where possible, and avoid leaving a scooter unattended for long periods.

## Charging and Maintenance Accessories

- **A spare battery** (on models that support it) extends your day.
- **A spare or travel charger** is useful if you split your time between homes.
- **A tyre pump and puncture repair kit** help with maintenance.
- **A cleaning cloth and mild cleaner** keep the scooter in good condition.

For battery care, see our [battery, range and charging tips](/blog/mobility-scooter-battery-range-charging-tips/).

## Transport Accessories

If you move your scooter by car, consider:

- **Ramps** suited to the scooter's weight and your vehicle's height.
- **Hoists or carriers** for heavier scooters.
- **Tie-down straps** to secure the scooter during transport.

Always check weight ratings and installation requirements, and seek professional advice for hoists and carriers fitted to vehicles.

## Buying Tips

1. **Check compatibility** with your scooter's make and model.
2. **Stay within weight limits** for baskets and carriers.
3. **Prioritise safety and visibility** over cosmetic extras.
4. **Choose quality materials** that suit Australian sun and weather.
5. **Ask your retailer** if an accessory could affect your warranty.
6. **Try before you buy** when comfort is the goal.
