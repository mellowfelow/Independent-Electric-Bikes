## Why the rest have none
- **Proprietary packs with no Australian price found** (Specialized U2 and SL packs, Giant EnergyPak 625, Trek Marlin+ CompactTube, Yamaha/Bafang batteries on Aventon, Fiido, NCM, DiroDi, Eunorau, Mokwheel, Lectric, Rad, Pedego, Super73): sold by the brand or dealer only, or not listed at an Australian retailer.
- **Electric unicycles**: chargers and tyres found only in USD (ewheels.com); no Australian listing.
- **Mobility scooters**: only US 12V 35Ah battery prices and generic AU ranges; no Australian listing tied to Pride, Shoprider, Merits or Drive models.
- **Hoverboards**: generic 36V 4.4Ah packs on eBay/Amazon with no confirmed fit per model.
- **Skateboards other than Evolve/Exway/Onewheel**: no AU part listings found (Meepo, Backfire, WowGo, Tynee).
- **Bosch bikes** are matched at drive-system level only: Smart System (2022+) vs older systems, and PowerPack vs PowerTube, depend on model year, which the catalogue does not record.
- Fit-by-size parts (tyres, tubes, rotors, pads) carry a fit rule, not a model list, because wheel size and brake mount are not recorded for catalogue models.
