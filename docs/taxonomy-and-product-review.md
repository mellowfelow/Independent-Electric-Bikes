## 2. Competitor comparison (qualitative)

- **PedL**: brand-led (Super73, Eunorau, Segway, Fiido) plus accessories and batteries.
- **Twelve Board Store**: strong on fat-tyre guides and electric boards; blog content drives traffic.
- **99 Bikes / Tribe**: heavy on cargo, commuter, parts and helmets.
- **Vamos**: single-brand with a folding focus.
- All mainstream competitors have a Parts/Accessories branch and a Batteries branch. Your site has neither.
